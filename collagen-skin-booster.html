Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Luxury Collagen Skin Booster?
It is a premium Italian medical-grade skin booster injected using an advanced 3-Layer Cellular Injection Technique. Unlike standard fillers, it provides no artificial volume — instead it stimulates your skin's own collagen and elastin, lifts naturally, brightens, and reduces pigmentation in a single treatment.
How does the 3-Layer Cellular Injection Technique work?
The treatment is injected into three different depths of the skin simultaneously, targeting fibroblasts to stimulate collagen production, repairing UV-related damage, reducing excess melanin, and deeply hydrating the skin — producing a visible lifting, brightening, and depigmenting effect in one session.
Who is this treatment suitable for?
This treatment is suitable for both men and women experiencing melasma, hyperpigmentation, sun damage, dull skin, early laxity, fine lines, dehydrated skin, or loss of elasticity. A free consultation with Dr. Orouji will confirm suitability for your specific skin concerns.
How fast will I see results?
Most clients notice improved hydration, glow, and a brighter complexion immediately after the first session. Full results — including reduced pigmentation, firmer skin, and visible lifting — develop over 2–3 months as collagen production accelerates.
How long do the results last?
Results typically last 12–18 months. The 3-Month Collagen Restoration Program (3 sessions) produces deeper, longer-lasting results. A simple top-up session maintains optimal skin quality without starting over.
Is the treatment painful?
Only mild discomfort. We apply a numbing cream before the procedure for maximum comfort. Most clients describe the sensation as minimal and tolerate it very well throughout the session.
